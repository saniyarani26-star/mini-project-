My Favorite Recipe - Pizza

Pizza is my favorite food. It is tasty and easy to make.

Ingredients

  • Pizza Base
  • Cheese
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Capsicum

Instructions

  1. Take a pizza base.
  2. Spread pizza sauce on it.
  3. Add vegetables and cheese.
  4. Bake the pizza.
  5. Serve hot and enjoy!
